# Shared Learning Vocabulary

[English](SHARED-LEARNING-VOCABULARY.md) | [Italiano](SHARED-LEARNING-VOCABULARY.it.md)

The cross-system learning vocabulary is:

```text
KNA          = Navigate
GYTE         = Prepare
VLE          = Verify
GiadaWare AI = Assist
```

For GYTE, **Prepare** means transforming source material into reviewed learning material and publication-ready derivatives while preserving the existing evidence, review, and editorial authority boundaries.

`Prepare` does not mean learner verification, mastery assessment, or authority over VLE evidence semantics.

`Navigate → Prepare → Verify` is a mental-model shorthand for a common conceptual flow, not a mandatory runtime pipeline or dependency order. GiadaWare AI remains optional and advisory.

The cross-system orientation record is maintained by GiadaWare KNA in `knowledge-navigation/shared-learning-vocabulary-v0.1.md`.
